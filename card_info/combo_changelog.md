### Added 4 Combos

#### archer - patron (Counter)
Making your opponent reveal Patrons in their hand while playing Archer will yield them Coffers.
#### banquet - quartermaster (Rush)
Opening double Banquet for Quartermasters (QM) followed by more QMs at every chance you get leaves you in a position where you can easily pick up lots of Silvers and rush for Provinces. Might play differently in a mirror as Estates and QMs might empty.
#### banquet - souk (Synergy)
Board-dependent synergy in terms of how aggressively you go for Souks, but exchanging your starting Estates for Coppers is certainly an accelerant.
#### desperation - quartermaster (Rush)
Opening double Quartermaster (QMs) with the help of Desperation and then a few more QMs during the second and third shuffle will position you well to pick up Provinces (don't hesitate to utilize Desperation for that as well). In a mirror, QMs, Estates and Curses might empty before any meaningful game can happen, so be careful.
