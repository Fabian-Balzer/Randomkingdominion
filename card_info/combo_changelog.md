### Added 26 Combos

#### amass - forum (Weak Synergy)
On your $4 or $5 opening hand, you can usually grab a free Forum along the way.
#### amass - nearby (Weak Synergy)
[Only if Nearby is on an Action] On your $4 or $5 opening hand, you can usually grab a free Nearby Action along the way, which is especially useful if it's on the stronger side.
#### bank - enlightenment (Weak Synergy)
With Enlightenment active, it's easy to put lots of Treasures into play, which will enable Banks played during the Buy phase to make lots of money.
#### bauble - siren (Weak Synergy)
Having played a Bauble for the topdecking option, you can topdeck Sirens without the need for trashing an Action.
#### bureaucracy - monkey (Counter)
Once Bureaucracy is active, Monkeys become stronger, as your opponents will gain more cards.
#### colonnade - innovation (Synergy)
Buying an Action and Innovating it immediately will allow you to get 2 VP off of Colonnade.
#### cultist - way_of_the_frog (Weak Synergy)
With Way of the Frog, you can chain your Cultists and play the last one non-terminally.
#### cultist - way_of_the_mule (Weak Synergy)
With Way of the Mule, you can chain your Cultists and play the last one non-terminally. However, giving your opponent Ruins might not do as much damage as they can always at least mule them.
#### cultist - way_of_the_ox (Weak Synergy)
With Way of the Ox, you can chain your Cultists and play the last one non-terminally. However, giving your opponent Ruins might not do as much damage as they can always at least ox them.
#### divine_wind - experiment (Combo)
Having Experiments in your deck while Divine Wind gets activated will allow you to keep them around as Laboratories after their pile has gone.
#### fools_gold - growth (Weak Synergy)
Be aware that once Growth is active, opponents with Fool's Golds in their hand might trash them in response to Province gains, enabling them to also gain Duchies.
#### gondola - sculptor (Weak Synergy)
Sometimes gaining Gondolas via Sculptor is the only Village available, but it might just be enough for your terminal needs.
#### governor - growth (Synergy)
Once Growth is active, gaining Gold off of Governors becomes more effective as you will be able to gain a $5 cost card while forcing your opponent (gaining Silver) to gain a $2 or lower cost card.
#### growth - skulk (Synergy)
Once Growth is active, gaining one Skulk will give you the ability to autopile Skulks, which can be used to facilitate a quick pileout. If there are any $5 cost kingdom Treasures such as Crystal Ball or Plunder, they can be piled out in the same gain-train.
#### guard_dog - way_of_the_turtle (Synergy)
Way of the Turtle makes Guard Dogs a very effective defense against discard Attacks such as Militia, as you'll be able to set them aside when reacting, enabling you to play them during your turn without consuming an Action, and starting from the reduced handsize.
#### hideout - watchtower (Weak Synergy)
Hideout trashing Estates would gain you Curses which you may trash with Watchtower in hand. On top of that, Hideout also is a handsize-decreasing Village, nice for playing Watchtower afterwards.
#### mapmaker - shaman (Synergy)
With Shamans around, it's very likely that you or your opponent will gain Estates at the start of your turns, turning Mapmakers into sifting Laboratories if you you have them in your opening hand.
#### merchant_guild - watchtower (Weak Synergy)
A Watchtower in hand can enable you to buy and immediately trash extra Coppers with your spare buys for more Merchant Guild coinage.
#### rebuild - trail (Weak Synergy)
Clearing your deck of green can sometimes enable you to discard lots of Trails. However, be aware that you're likely to trigger a shuffle when doing so, in which case you'll only be able to play one Trail, loosing the on-discard ability of the others..
#### rebuild - village_green (Weak Synergy)
Clearing your deck of green can sometimes enable you to discard lots of Village Greens. However, be aware that you're likely to trigger a shuffle when doing so, in which case you'll only be able to play one (usually the last) Village Green for this turn, while the others should be next-turned.
#### rebuild - weaver (Weak Synergy)
Clearing your deck of green can sometimes enable you to discard lots of Weavers, gaining lots of cards in the process.
#### ronin - tactician (Weak Synergy)
Ronin offers a nice and reliable way to draw back up to 7 cards after having played Tactician, especially pleasant with Action-based payload and good Village space.
#### shaman - transmogrify (Synergy)
With Shamans around, you sometimes can immediately gain back valuable cards trashed for Transmogrify at the start of your turn.
#### trail - way_of_the_butterfly (Weak Synergy)
With Trail and Butterfly around, by buying Trail with $4, you can immediately Butterfly it to open with a $5 cost card instead.
#### villa - way_of_the_butterfly (Weak Synergy)
With Villa and Butterfly around, by buying Villa with $4, you can immediately Butterfly it to open with a $5 cost card instead.
#### villa - way_of_the_owl (Synergy)
Buying a Villa after playing all of your Treasures and then Owling it offers a great way to draw. Just be careful you account for the extra Buy you might need :)
