### Added 13 Interactions

#### baker - divine_wind
If Baker is added to the kingdom once Divine Wind is triggered, everyone gets +1 coffers.
#### canal - plateau_shepherds
Since Canal only reduces the cost of cards during your turns, at the end of the game, it doesn't reduce the cost of cards as far as Plateau Shepherds is concerned.
#### charlatan - divine_wind
In games where Charlatan is part of the initial kingdom, Curses are also Treasures worth $1 even after Divine Wind has removed it from the Supply. If Charlatan is instead part of the Divine Wind kingdom, the additional rule comes online once Divine Wind has been triggered.
#### cheap - plateau_shepherds
[Relevant if Cheap is on $3 or $2 cost card] If Cheap is assigned to a card initially costing $3 (like e.g. Underling), it becomes a $2 cost card as far as Plateau Shepherds (PS) is concerned. Conversely, a card (say, Bauble) reduced to costing $1 due to Cheap will not be eligible for PS anymore.
#### defiled_shrine - divine_wind
If Defiled Shrine is present, once Divine Wind triggers, no additional VP chips are added onto the new piles (since Defiled Shrine itself is not newly added).
#### divine_wind - footpad
In games where Footpad is part of the initial kingdom, you still get +1 Card for every card gained during an Action phase even after Divine Wind has removed it from the Supply. If Footpad is instead part of the Divine Wind kingdom, the additional rule comes online once Divine Wind has been triggered.
#### divine_wind - reckless
As all Traits, Reckless will still affect its pile even after Divine Wind has been triggered. However, due to the Reckless pile not being part of the kingdom anymore, you then cannot return cards from it during cleanup, and they will stay within your deck (getting discarded during cleanup instead).
#### divine_wind - shaman
In games where Shaman is part of the initial kingdom, you still gain a card costing up to $6 from the trash at the beginning of your turn even after Divine Wind has removed it from the Supply. If Shaman is instead part of the Divine Wind kingdom, the additional rule comes online once Divine Wind has been triggered.
#### divine_wind - tax
If Tax is present, once Divine Wind triggers, no additional Debt tokens are added onto the new piles (since Tax itself is not newly added).
#### ferry - plateau_shepherds
Since Ferry only reduces the cost of cards during your turns, at the end of the game, the Ferry token does not reduce the cost of cards as far as Plateau Shepherds is concerned.
#### fisherman - plateau_shepherds
At the end of the game, Fisherman costs $5 and is thus not eligible for Plateau Shepherds.
#### flourishing_trade - plateau_shepherds
If Flourishing Trade is active by the end of the game, the new (reduced) cost of cards will be considered for Plateau Shepherds (PS), so you would e.g. be awarded PS points for Silvers, but not for Estates.
#### fools_gold - reckless
[Only if Reckless is on Fool's Gold] A Reckless Fool's Gold played as the first Fool's Gold will only yield $2 in total.
