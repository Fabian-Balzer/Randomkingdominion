#### armory - inn
[Only with Cost Reduction] If you gain an Inn onto your deck using Armory (enabled by Cost Reduction), it is first topdecked, but then your deck will be shuffled afterwards.
#### artificer - inn

#### bauble - inn
If you gain an Inn and have the option to topdeck it using Bauble, you can choose the order. If you choose to shuffle in Action cards first, you can only topdeck it afterwards if you haven't shuffled the Inn itself in. If you choose to topdeck the Inn before shuffling Action cards in, your deck will be shuffled afterwards and it won't stay on top.
